# UK holiday rules

Reference for statutory minimums in Great Britain. Check against GOV.UK before relying on it, since the rules change. Northern Ireland has its own regulations.

## Statutory minimum
- 5.6 weeks of paid leave per leave year.
- For a 5-day week that is 28 days, which is also the statutory cap: someone working 6 days a week still gets 28.
- Part-time workers get 5.6 times their usual working week (e.g. 3 days a week gives 16.8 days).
- Employers can include bank holidays in the 5.6 weeks. There is no statutory right to bank holidays off on top.

## Irregular-hours and part-year workers
- For leave years starting on or after 1 April 2024, holiday accrues at 12.07% of hours worked in each pay period.
- Rolled-up holiday pay is allowed for these workers.

## Starters and leavers
- In the first year, leave can accrue at 1/12 of the annual entitlement per month worked.
- On leaving, untaken statutory leave must be paid in lieu.

## Carry-over
- 4 weeks of the entitlement (the EU-derived part) can only be carried over in specific cases: sickness, family leave, or the employer stopping the worker from taking it.
- The extra 1.6 weeks can be carried over if the contract allows it.
